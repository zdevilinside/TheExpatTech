# Residency in Mexico: Financial Requirements

It is now 2026 and Mexico has increased the minimum wage and financial solvency requirements for residency.  The days of just walking to the border and living in Mexico are gone.  They now require you to get residency if you wish to live here.  If you come in on a FMM (Tourist Card) and are caught living in Mexico on it, you can be kicked out of the country, and have your passport marked "not valid for entry into Mexico" according to Sonia Diaz, a San Miguel-based immigration attorney.

The amounts have increased to such a point that Mexico is no longer the bargain it used to be before. Over the past five years, residency requirements have risen sharply. Temporary residency thresholds have nearly doubled, increasing by about **75–100%** compared to 2021. Permanent residency requirements have grown even more steeply: at the Tucson consulate, they are up by roughly **200%**, tripling the income and savings levels expected of applicants. At the London consulate, where requirements are expressed in UMA, the increase has been about **150–170%**, reflecting both inflation and adjustments to UMA values. These changes have outpaced Mexico’s minimum wage growth (about **120%** over the same period), underscoring how much more difficult it has become to qualify for residency today.

## Quick Comparison

**Temporary Residency**  
Requirement Type: Monthly income / savings  
Thresholds: ~$4,185 USD/month OR ~$69,750 USD savings (12 months).  

**Permanent Residency (Tucson)**  
Requirement Type: Savings / Pension income  
Thresholds: Savings: $292,859 USD (12 months avg) OR Pension: $7,322 USD/month.  

**Permanent Residency (London)**  
Requirement Type: Savings / Income in UMA  
Thresholds: Savings: 45,850 UMA (12 months avg) OR Income: 1,140 UMA/month (6 months).  

**Minimum Wage (2026 – General Zone)**  
Requirement Type: Daily wage  
Thresholds: MXN $315.04/day (~MXN $9,451.20/month).  

**Minimum Wage (2026 – Border Zone)**  
Requirement Type: Daily wage  
Thresholds: MXN $440.87/day (~MXN $13,226.10/month).  

> **Note:** All amounts are shown in the currency used in the original consular or wage sources (USD for U.S. consulates, UMA units or GBP-equivalent for UK consulate references, MXN pesos for minimum wage data).  
> **Note:** Income or savings benchmarks vary by consulate.

## Temporary Residency

Applicants must demonstrate **monthly income or savings/investments** that meet the updated thresholds.  
- **Income:** ~$4,185 USD/month (last 6 months).  
- **Savings:** ~$69,750 USD average balance over 12 months.  
- Proof must be provided through **bank statements, investment records, or pension documentation**.  
- Requirements vary slightly by consulate (Tucson, Orlando, Calexico, etc.).

---

## Permanent Residency

### Tucson Consulate (U.S./Canada Applicants)

- **Investments or savings:** Average monthly balance of **$292,859 USD** over the past 12 months.  
- **Pension income:** Monthly pension of at least **$7,322 USD**.  
- **Fee:** $56 USD (non-refundable).

### London Consulate (UK/Other Nationals)

- **Investments or savings:** Equivalent to **45,850 UMA days** averaged over the past 12 months.  
- **Income/pension:** Monthly income greater than **1,140 UMA days** for at least 6 months.  

#### What is UMA?

The **Unidad de Medida y Actualización (UMA)** is an inflation-indexed unit used in Mexico to calculate obligations such as fines, taxes, and residency requirements.  
- UMA daily value (2026): **MXN $108.84**.  
- **45,850 UMA ≈ MXN $4,987,314** (savings requirement).  
- **1,140 UMA ≈ MXN $124,098/month** (income requirement).

## Minimum Wage in Mexico (2026)

| Zone                          | 2025 Daily Wage | 2026 Daily Wage | % Increase | Approx. Monthly Equivalent* |
|-------------------------------|-----------------|-----------------|------------|-----------------------------|
| **General Zone**              | MXN $278.80     | MXN $315.04     | **13%**    | ~MXN $9,451.20              |
| **Northern Border Free Zone** | MXN $419.88     | MXN $440.87     | **5%**     | ~MXN $13,226.10             |

\*Monthly equivalents are calculated by multiplying the daily rate × 30 days. Mexico legally defines wages per day, not per month.

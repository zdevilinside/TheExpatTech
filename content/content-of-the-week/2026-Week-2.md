## Ugreen Enters the AI NAS Arena

Ugreen is stepping into the high‑end NAS market with a new line of AI‑accelerated storage boxes powered by Intel Core Ultra processors. Think “Google Photos‑style search,” but fully offline.

### **Pricing**
- iDX6011 (32GB RAM): $1,699  
- iDX6011 (64GB RAM): $1,999  
- iDX6011 Pro (64GB RAM): $2,599  

Storage not included. Supports:  
- Up to 6× SATA HDDs  
- 2× NVMe SSDs  
- Max capacity: 196TB  

The Pro model uses the faster **Core Ultra 7 255H**; the standard model uses the **Ultra 5 125H**.

### **AI Features**
- **Universal Search** (natural language queries like “photos of Dad on a bike”)  
- **Uliya AI Chat** — an offline LLM for summarizing documents, answering questions, and acting as a private knowledge base  
- **AI Album** — face, object, scene, and text recognition  
- **Voice Memos** — transcription, translation, summarization  
- **AI File Organization** — automatic sorting by type, date, and name  

### **Design & Build**
- Aluminum chassis  
- Built‑in PSU  
- Dual 10GbE  
- 2× Thunderbolt 4  
- HDMI  
- Full‑size SD slot  
- Internal PCIe x8  
- Pro model adds a touchscreen + OCuLink for external GPU support  

For more details, visit The Verge: https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/852453/ugreen-ai-nas-idx6011-pro-photo-search

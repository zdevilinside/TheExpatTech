## 🥁 My Frank Beard Experience

When my family moved to the suburbs of Houston, I went out for a walk and saw a guy working on a blue sports car in his garage.  I ask if I could look at the car, and was waved up to visit.  We were talking about the car (Ferrari 308GTB quatrovalve) and talked about various things.  I ask the guy his name (Rube) and I noticed something - I could have sworn I had seen him before.  I state that and Rube says "I hear that all the time." I notice the clock over his shoulder and ask if the clock was right.  He said it was and I told him I was late getting home and took off.

A few days later, I was watching MTV when ZZ Top's Legs video comes on and I recognize "Rube".  He is Frank Beard, the drummer.  The next time I saw him, I said he was the drummer for ZZ Top.  He looked at me for a second and said, "No, I am Rube.  I am a lot more than ZZ Top's drummer."  We talked a bit more about golf and his house being on the fairway on one of Quail Valley's golf courses.  (I don't remember which of the 4 it was on nor the hole #.)  I do remember the house was on Robinson Road.

A few months later, I got to the bus stop and one of my classmates asked me why I wasn't in uniform - I was a member of the AFJROTC unit at Dulles High School.  I forgot they changed uniform day, so I ran back home to change into my uniform.  As I changed, the bus arrived and I missed it.  I decided to run "cross country" to the last stop on the bus route in time to see it turning the corner off Robinson Road.

I started walking home and a guy in a nice Mercedes pulls up to me rolling down the window.  It was Rube!  He commented that I missed the bus.  I said, "Yeah, I have to go home and call my Mom to see if she would come and get me so I could be taken to school."  Rube said not to worry, that he would give me a ride.  He has me get in and we start heading to Dulles High.

About 3 blocks down the road, he pulls over, says he was going to give me a ride in style.  He hangs a u-turn, and we go back to his house.  He takes me to school in his Ferrari.  When I got out of the car at school, people were asking me if I knew who it was that gave me a ride.  My answer "My neighbor and friend, Rube."

Rube has continued his Ferrai collection over the years.  I found a photo of his car collection of a few years back.  The 308 GTB I got a ride in is the blue car on the left side of this photo.

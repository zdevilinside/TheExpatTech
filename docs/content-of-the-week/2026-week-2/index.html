Week 2 - CES Madness

CES 2026 — A Wild Week in Tech

CES wrapped up in Las Vegas this week, and as always, it felt like Christmas morning for electronics fans. There were far too many product announcements to cover them all, but these are the ones that stood out the most to me.

GL.iNet Drops Two Heavy Hitters: Mudi 7 & Flint 4

GL.iNet announced two new devices that immediately caught my attention: the Mudi 7 and the Flint 4.

Mudi 7 (GL‑E5800)

This one is going to be the travel router to get. It brings:

  • Tri‑band Wi‑Fi 7
  • Dual SIM + eSIM support
  • Long‑life battery
  • Cellular capability baked in

I can’t comment on anything beyond the public specs, but NASCompares did a great overview that aligns with my impressions. Let’s just say that this device will be THE travel router to get.

Product page: https://www.gl-inet.com/products/gl-e5800/

Flint 4

This router is a beast:

  • 10G + 2.5G ports
  • Touchscreen
  • Dual WAN (10GbE + 2.5GbE)
  • 3× 2.5GbE + 4× 1GbE LAN
  • SFP slot (usable instead of the 10GbE WAN)
  • Tri‑band Wi‑Fi 7
  • USB‑C and USB 3.0

I already told one of my GL.iNet contacts I’m buying one the moment it becomes available. He laughed and said it is in prototype stage. NASCompares is the same — TAKE MY MONEY!!!!

Ugreen Enters the AI NAS Arena

Ugreen is stepping into the high‑end NAS market with a new line of AI‑accelerated storage boxes powered by Intel Core Ultra processors. Think “Google Photos‑style search,” but fully offline.

Pricing

  • iDX6011 (32GB RAM): $1,699
  • iDX6011 (64GB RAM): $1,999
  • iDX6011 Pro (64GB RAM): $2,599

Storage not included. Supports:

  • Up to 6× SATA HDDs
  • 2× NVMe SSDs
  • Max capacity: 196TB

The Pro model uses the faster Core Ultra 7 255H; the standard model uses the Ultra 5 125H.

AI Features

  • Universal Search (natural language queries like “photos of Dad on a bike”)
  • Uliya AI Chat — an offline LLM for summarizing documents, answering questions, and acting as a private knowledge base
  • AI Album — face, object, scene, and text recognition
  • Voice Memos — transcription, translation, summarization
  • AI File Organization — automatic sorting by type, date, and name

Design & Build

  • Aluminum chassis
  • Built‑in PSU
  • Dual 10GbE
  • 2× Thunderbolt 4
  • HDMI
  • Full‑size SD slot
  • Internal PCIe x8
  • Pro model adds a touchscreen + OCuLink for external GPU support

For more details, visit The Verge: https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/852453/ugreen-ai-nas-idx6011-pro-photo-search

GL.iNet’s Comet 5G — Remote Access Even When the Internet Dies

If you need access to systems even during an outage, GL.iNet’s Comet 5G is worth a look. It’s essentially a Comet Pro with added 5G RedCap cellular capability and a 3.69" touchscreen. I originally didn’t think there was a market for this device but it has grown on me. I now think it is going to be a very good product to have.
